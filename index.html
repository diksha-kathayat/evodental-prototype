Not a Diagnosis
The information, digital tools and smile visualisations in this app do not replace a clinical examination, your Full Case Assessment (FCA), or professional dental advice.

Follow Your Clinical Team
Always follow the advice and instructions provided by your EVO Dental surgeon and care team regarding your treatment and recovery.

Medical Emergencies
If you experience severe bleeding, persistent fever, significant swelling or any urgent concern after surgery, contact your EVO Dental clinic or your local emergency services immediately. Do not rely on the app for emergency care.
