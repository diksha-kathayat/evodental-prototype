What are full mouth implants?

Full mouth dental implant treatment is where a one-piece set of artificial teeth is placed onto titanium implants embedded in the jawbone. The teeth are fixed in place and won't fall out or need to be removed.

Is this treatment right for you?

Full mouth dental implant treatment is designed for people whose quality of life is impacted by failing, missing teeth, dentures or gum disease.

  • You limit the foods you eat because of your teeth or dentures
  • You feel embarrassed or self-conscious about your smile
  • Your oral health is affecting your overall quality of life
  • You want a long-term, secure solution
